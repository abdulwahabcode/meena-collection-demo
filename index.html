About Meena Collection

A boutique built on craft & trust

Meena Collection is a fashion boutique in the heart of Meena Bazar, Peshawar. We specialise in Afghani and traditional dresses — pieces that carry the embroidery, colour and character of our heritage, made for the women who wear them today.

Every day, new designs go up on our Instagram for our 76,000+ followers. Every dress is made to order: your colour, your design, your size. And wherever you are in the world, we ship it to you.

“Apni pasand ka rang, apna design — hum aapke liye banayenge.”
